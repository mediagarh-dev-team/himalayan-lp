About Himalayan Progressive School

A School That Builds Strong Foundations for the Future

Established in 2003, Himalayan Progressive School is a co-educational, English-medium school located on Nainital Road, Kichha, District Udham Singh Nagar, Uttarakhand.

The school aims to provide a positive and stimulating environment where students can enjoy learning, develop their potential and become independent learners.

With an emphasis on academic growth, integrity, ethics, responsibility and self-discipline, HPS encourages students to develop the knowledge and values that support them throughout life.

Defence Preparation with Himalayan Defence Academy

For Those Who Aspire to Serve in the Armed Forces

A career in the Armed Forces requires dedication, preparation and an understanding of the selection process.

Himalayan Defence Academy, built on the foundation of Himalayan Progressive School, focuses on defence-oriented preparation and guidance for aspiring candidates.

CDS Preparation

Preparation for graduates interested in the Combined Defence Services examination.

SSB Interview Training

Guidance covering aspects of the Services Selection Board process, including psychological tests, group tasks, personal interviews and communication skills.
